Assistant Professor of Economics · Northwestern University

SilviaVannutelli

I am an applied economist. I study governments as complex organizations: how institutional design, political incentives, and organizational structures shape government decisions and public-sector performance.

What I work on

Original data, rigorous methods, policy questions

I examine both the forces that lead policymakers to adopt particular policies and the effects of those choices on individuals, firms, and communities.

Political Economy

Accountability, political selection, and the incentives behind policy design.

Public Economics

Taxes, transfers, audits, and how governments raise and spend money.

Organizational Economics

Monitoring, discretion, and personnel inside public organizations.
