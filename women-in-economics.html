04 · Community

Women in Economics

  1. Northwestern · Advisor, 2021–present

    Northwestern Womxn in Economics

    I regularly participate in events organized by Northwestern Womxn in Economics (WiE), an undergraduate student-led organization seeking to uplift and empower underrepresented genders in economics.

    I held a mini-course about pathways to getting a PhD and the opportunities offered by PREDOC.

    Mini-course slides
  2. During my PhD

    Seminar Dynamics Collective

    I was part of the Seminar Dynamics Collective, a group of almost 100 economists (mostly graduate students) who volunteered to analyze seminar dynamics and collect and code data for the paper “Gender and the Dynamics of Economics Seminars” by Pascaline Dupas, Alicia Sasser Modestino, Muriel Niederle, Justin Wolfers and the Seminar Dynamics Collective.

    NBER paper My CSWEP Newsletter article
  3. Boston University · Co-Chair, 2017–2019

    BU Women in Economics (WEOrg)

    I served as Co-Chair of BU WEOrg, a graduate student-led organization dedicated to the advancement of women in all stages of economic research.

    BU WEOrg website weorg@bu.edu
  4. Summer 2019 · Organizer

    WERISE Conference

    We organized WERISE (Women in Economics: Research, Ideas, Solutions, Executions), a conference bringing together leading scholars for a comprehensive overview of research on the status of women in economics, to reach a deeper understanding of the challenges women face in the profession and to spur ideas for concrete solutions.
