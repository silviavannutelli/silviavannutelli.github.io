G3O Principal Investigator

Global Government GenAI Observatory

Documenting and studying the use of generative AI across public institutions worldwide.

Brief description. A few sentences on this project.

G3O builds a versioned, open-source panel of institution-level observations that make public-sector GenAI use visible, comparable, and auditable at scale.

  • Documents evidence, tools, vendors, safeguards, and governance practices.
  • Supports cumulative research and evidence-based policy.
  • Promotes transparency, reproducibility, and auditability.

A project by researchers at Stanford University, Northwestern University, and the University of Rochester.
